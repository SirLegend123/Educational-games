🚀 Standard Form Space Race

Fly your rocket from the size of an atom ⚛️ all the way out to the size of our galaxy 🌌. At each stop there's a standard form mission. Complete it to fuel up and blast off to the next stop!

Standard form reminder

  • A number in standard form looks like a × 10n, where 1 ≤ a < 10 and n is a whole number.
  • Big numbers have positive powers: 384 400 000 = 3.844 × 108.
  • Small numbers (less than 1) have negative powers: 0.000008 = 8 × 10−6.
  • To compare numbers in standard form, look at the powers first. If they're the same, compare the front numbers.
