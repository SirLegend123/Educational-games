✳️ Line Up

A two-player game about straight lines and where they cross. Take turns drawing lines with equations like y = 2x + 1 to claim stars, then work out where your line crosses your opponent's.

How to play

  1. The grid has 8 stars. Players take turns drawing one line each, by typing its equation.
  2. If your line goes through a free star, you claim it (1 point).
  3. If your line goes through one of your opponent's stars, it crosses their line right on the star, so you steal it! A stolen star is locked and can't be stolen back.
  4. After drawing your line, work out exactly where it crosses your opponent's latest line. Type the crossing point, or say the lines are parallel. A correct answer scores 2 points.
  5. The player with the most points after all the rounds wins.
