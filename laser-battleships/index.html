⚡ Laser Battleships

A two-player game about straight-line graphs. Hide your ships, then try to sink your opponent's fleet by firing lasers. To fire, type the equation of a straight line in the form y = mx + c.

How to play

  1. Each player secretly places two ships on a coordinate grid. Pass the device between turns and don't peek! (Or play solo against the computer.)
  2. On your turn, type a line such as y = 2x + 1 and press Fire. Your laser follows that line across your opponent's grid.
  3. Every time a laser crosses a side of a ship, it makes a hole. Going right through a ship (in one side and out another) makes 2 holes. Only touching a corner or running along an edge (a graze) makes 1 hole.
  4. A ship sinks when it has 4 holes. Holes show up on the grid, so use them to aim your next laser!
  5. The first player to sink both enemy ships wins.
