🚗 Parallel Parking

Park your car by typing the equation of the line it should drive along. Each level shows a road and a parking spot P. Your line must go through P and be either parallel or perpendicular to the road.

Remember

  • Parallel lines have the same gradient. A line parallel to y = 3x + 1 has gradient 3.
  • Perpendicular lines meet at right angles. Their gradients multiply to make −1, so flip the fraction and change the sign. A line perpendicular to y = 2x + 1 has gradient −1/2.
  • To find c, put the coordinates of P into y = mx + c and solve.
  • In later levels the road's equation is hidden. Work out its gradient from two marked points: change in y ÷ change in x.

10 levels. Earn ⭐⭐⭐ by parking first time without hints.
