How to expand a bracket

  • Multiply every term inside the bracket by the term outside: 3(x + 4) = 3 × x + 3 × 4 = 3x + 12.
  • Watch the signs: −2(x − 5) = −2 × x + (−2) × (−5) = −2x + 10. A negative times a negative is a positive!
  • x × x = x², so x(x + 3) = x² + 3x.
  • Expand and simplify: expand each bracket, then collect like terms. With 5(x + 1) − 2(x − 3), the −2 multiplies both terms in the second bracket.
  • Type answers like 3x + 12 or x^2 + 3x (or use ² for squared).
