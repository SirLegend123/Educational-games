⭕ Noughts and Numbers

Noughts and crosses, but with numbers! Instead of X and O, each player places digits. Be the one to complete a line that adds up to exactly 10.

How to play

  • Each player has the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and can use each one only once per game.
  • Take turns: choose an empty square, then one of your unused digits.
  • When a row, column or diagonal is full and its three numbers total exactly 10, the player who completed it wins. Both players' digits count!
  • Think ahead: if a line has two numbers totalling 6, the next player can win with a 4, but only if they still have a 4.
  • If the board fills up with no line of 10, it's a draw.
  • The running totals turn yellow when one more digit could make that line 10, so watch out!
