🏦 Standard Form Bank Heist

Twelve vaults, each locked with a standard form calculation. Crack the code to grab the loot, but a wrong answer sets off the alarm! 🚨

The safe-cracker's notes

  • ×: multiply the front numbers, add the powers. (3 × 10⁴) × (4 × 10⁵) = 12 × 10⁹
  • ÷: divide the front numbers, subtract the powers. (8 × 10⁹) ÷ (2 × 10³) = 4 × 10⁶
  • Adjust! The front number must be at least 1 and less than 10: 12 × 10⁹ = 1.2 × 10¹⁰, and 0.25 × 10⁶ = 2.5 × 10⁵.
  • + and −: the powers must match first. 4 × 10⁵ + 3 × 10⁴ = 4 × 10⁵ + 0.3 × 10⁵ = 4.3 × 10⁵. You can also write them as ordinary numbers.

Right first time: full loot 💰. Right on the second try: half. A wrong answer gets a hint, and a second wrong answer shows the working.
