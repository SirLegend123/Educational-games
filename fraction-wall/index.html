🧱 Fraction Wall Race

Build a fraction wall as fast as you can. Every row has to add up to exactly 1 whole: no gaps and no overhang!

How to play

  • Each row starts with one or two bricks already in place. Tap bricks from the tray to fill the rest of the row.
  • The running total shows your working, e.g. 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
  • If a brick would make the row more than 1, it won't fit and you get a +5 second penalty. Tap a brick you placed to take it back off.
  • There are 10 rows, and they get trickier as you build higher. How fast can you finish?
