🎓 Educational Games

Free browser games for practising maths and more. No installation needed, just pick a game and play.

⚡

Laser Battleships

Hide ships on a coordinate grid and sink them by firing lasers along straight lines, y = mx + c. Play a friend or the computer.

Maths · Gradient & y-intercept · 1 or 2 players
✳️

Line Up

Draw lines to claim stars, steal your opponent's stars by crossing their lines, and work out exactly where lines meet.

Maths · Simultaneous equations · 2 players
🚗

Parallel Parking

Park your car on a line parallel or perpendicular to the road, through the parking spot. 10 levels to earn stars.

Maths · Parallel & perpendicular gradients · 1 player
🚪

Equation Escape Room

Solve an equation to unlock each door. Ten rooms, from one-step equations to brackets on both sides. Beat the clock!

Maths · Solving linear equations · 1 player
🏰

Prime Factor Defence

Number monsters march on your castle. Blast them with primes until they're divided down to 1, and see their prime factorisation.

Maths · Prime factors & index notation · 1 player
🧱

Fraction Wall Race

Race the clock to build a fraction wall where every row adds up to exactly 1. No gaps, no overhang!

Maths · Adding & equivalent fractions · 1 player
🚀

Standard Form Space Race

Fly from the size of an atom to the size of the galaxy. Write, read, fix and order real sizes in standard form to fuel your rocket.

Maths · Standard form · 1 player
