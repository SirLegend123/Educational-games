🎯 Sequence Sniper

Numbers are flying past! Each round has a rule like 4n − 1. Tap only the numbers that are terms of that sequence.

Is it in the sequence?

  • Set the rule equal to the number and solve. For 4n − 1 and 99: 4n − 1 = 99, so 4n = 100 and n = 25. That's a whole number, so 99 is the 25th term ✅
  • For 150: 4n − 1 = 150, so 4n = 151 and n = 37.75. That's not a whole number, so 150 is not in the sequence ❌
  • n must be a positive whole number (1, 2, 3…). For 4n − 1, −1 would be "term 0", which doesn't exist ❌
  • Tapping a term: +10 (+5 bonus for terms beyond the 20th). Tapping a number that isn't a term costs a ❤️. You have 3.
  • 5 rounds of 30 seconds, with a new rule each round. Terms that fly past untouched count as missed.
