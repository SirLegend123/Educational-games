🏔️ Mixed Number Mountain

Climb to the summit! Each checkpoint has a calculation with mixed numbers. The higher you go, the harder it gets: converting, then adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing.

Climbing kit

  • Improper fractions are your rope. Change mixed numbers to improper fractions first: 2⅓ = (2 × 3 + 1)/3 = 7/3.
  • + and −: make the denominators the same, then add or subtract the numerators.
  • ×: multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. Don't multiply the whole numbers and fractions separately!
  • ÷: Keep the first fraction, Change ÷ to ×, Flip the second fraction.
  • Give answers as a mixed number in its simplest form. Type a space between the whole number and the fraction, e.g. 3 5/6. You can also type 11/4 or 4.

Right first time: ⭐⭐. Right on the second try: ⭐. A wrong answer gets a hint, and a second wrong answer shows the working.
