🏰 Prime Factor Defence

Number monsters are marching on your castle! Blast them with prime numbers to break them down. A monster is defeated when it's been divided all the way down to 1.

How to play

  • Fire a prime by tapping its button or pressing its key: 2 3 5 7, or 11 for 11 and 13 for 13.
  • Your laser hits the targeted monster (dashed gold ring). That's the one at the front, unless you tap a different one.
  • If the prime is a factor, the monster's number is divided by it. Example: 60 → hit with 2 → 30.
  • If it's not a factor, the laser bounces off and the monster speeds up!
  • When a monster reaches 1 it's defeated, and you see its prime factorisation, e.g. 60 = 2² × 3 × 5.
  • If a monster reaches the castle you lose a ❤️. Survive as many waves as you can!
